# Codelume Privacy Policy

**Version: 2026.11.28**

**Operator: Beijing Xingjieli Technology Co., Ltd.** (“we”, “us”, or “our”)

We value your privacy. This policy explains how Codelume and related online services collect, use, store, and protect your information.

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Accounts, submissions and interactions
- When you use Sign in with Apple, we verify your identity through Supabase and process the account identifier and any email address Apple supplies, including a relay address if you choose one. The app stores the login session in the macOS Keychain.
- Your chosen display name, bio, avatar and background appear on your profile. When you submit a wallpaper, the selected bundle, previews and work details are uploaded for review and distribution. We record likes, follows, downloads and reports. When you agree to a policy, the server records your account ID, the policy version ID and the time of acceptance.
- When you contact support, we handle the contact details and message you provide. For purchases, we process transaction identifiers, amounts, refund status and account entitlements to reconcile orders or restore access.

### 1.2 Local wallpapers and optional permissions
- Imported wallpapers, playlists, display configurations and preview caches are stored on your Mac. Submitting a work to the community uploads the content you select for that submission. Storage settings let you inspect and clear local resources.
- When you enable system audio input, a sound-reactive wallpaper can analyze the audio playing on your Mac locally to drive visual effects. This feature uses system audio output. Interactive wallpapers can use pointer position and primary clicks for parallax or click effects.
- If you enable calendar access and grant system permission, the wallpaper engine can use event titles and time intervals from the calendars you select to change the scene. You can change the selection or turn access off.
- In builds that offer weather wallpapers, you can choose a place manually or grant permission to obtain device coordinates. The location is stored locally and used with Apple location and weather services. You can manage the relevant permissions in the app or in macOS System Settings.

### 1.3 Network requests
Online features send the information needed to handle a request, such as IP address, request time, wallpaper ID and sign-in status. We use it to deliver files, verify access, troubleshoot errors and prevent abuse. Avoid adding government identification, payment card details or other sensitive information to public profiles or submissions.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve wallpaper browsing, downloading, submission, review, and account features;
- Authenticate users and manage security risks (e.g. preventing bulk scraping or upload abuse);
- Comply with legal obligations and handle complaints and infringement notices;
- Send important service-related notices where you have consented.

## 3. Sharing and processors

We do not sell your personal information. Apple handles Sign in with Apple and any optional Apple services you use. Supabase provides authentication and database services. Cloudflare hosts the website and stores and delivers wallpaper bundles and public previews. Where there is a payment or refund, Paddle handles payment details and transaction information. Other users can view works, previews and public profile information you publish.

We use these providers as needed to deliver the corresponding service and may disclose necessary information to competent authorities where required by law.

## 4. Storage and cross-border transfer

Local wallpapers and settings are stored on your Mac. Our online services and cloud providers process account, submission and interaction data. Service locations may be outside your country or region. We keep records for as long as needed for the service, security, transactions and legal obligations. You can request account deletion in the app. Once the request is accepted, the account enters a pending-deletion state and signed-in sessions are invalidated.

## 5. Your rights

Where applicable law allows, you may:

- Access and correct your account information (in the app or by email);
- Withdraw certain consents (some features may become unavailable);
- Request deletion of your account and related personal data (subject to legal exceptions);
- Ask questions or file complaints about this policy.

Email **codelume@163.com** and we will respond within a reasonable period.

## 6. Minors

Codelume is intended primarily for adults. Minors should read this policy and use the service with a guardian. If we learn we collected a minor’s information without guardian consent, we will delete it promptly.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website (codelume.cn) may use browser local storage or similar technologies to remember language preferences and maintain sessions. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

## 8. Policy updates

We keep versioned copies on our website. When we require fresh consent, the app displays the new version when you sign in or when it restores your session at startup. After reading it, you can agree or sign out. The server records acceptance of that version only when you submit your agreement.
